PrivacyNotch

Smart, private notch hub

Turn your MacBook notch into a tiny hub: system stats, music, calendar, reminders, a file shelf and a focus timer. Hides itself when you share your screen.

Coming soon to the App Store

PrivacyNotch turns your MacBook notch into a useful, discreet "Dynamic Island". Everything you want at a glance sits right next to the camera, and private content only shows when it is really you looking.

Notch and hub

  • Wings on both sides of the notch: CPU, GPU, RAM, network speed, disk read/write speed, now playing, focus timer and your next event.
  • Hover or click the notch to open the Hub: detailed stats, Music and Spotify controls, today's calendar, reminders, a drag-and-drop file shelf and a focus timer.
  • Turn each part on or off in Settings.
  • Mac without a notch: a floating island at the top center of the screen.

Private by default

  • Reminders, calendar alerts and timers that PrivacyNotch shows on the notch stay covered until it confirms the owner is looking.
  • The private file shelf only opens when it is really you.
  • Two faces looking at the screen: content is covered right away.
  • Sharing or mirroring your screen: the notch hides itself, viewers see nothing.
  • No camera, or the camera is busy: unlock with Touch ID or your Mac password.

Your face stays on your mac

  • The camera turns on for less than 0.3 seconds only when verification is needed, and the green camera light still shows.
  • No photos are stored. Only a face feature vector is kept in this Mac's Keychain, never synced to iCloud, never sent anywhere. Delete it at any time.
  • No ads, no analytics, no account.

Convenient

  • Opens at login and lives quietly in the menu bar.
  • Pause covering for 1 hour with a single click.
  • Vietnamese and English.
  • Requires macOS 14 or later.
